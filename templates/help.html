Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding flight bookings, baggage policy, online check-in, and AeroRewards.

❓ How do I complete my online check-in and get a boarding pass?

Online check-in opens 48 hours before scheduled departure. Simply visit the Check-in Page, enter your 6-character PNR (e.g. AX7K9P) and passenger last name, then click 'View & Print Boarding Pass'.

💳 Is real money charged during the payment step?

No! Aeronexa Airways is a software engineering portfolio application. All payments use sandbox demo simulation with dynamic QR code payloads. No real bank accounts or UPI credentials are ever accessed.

🧳 What is the checked baggage allowance for Economy & Business class?

Economy Saver includes 7kg cabin baggage. Economy Flex includes 23kg checked baggage. Business Premier includes 32kg checked baggage + priority handling. You can purchase additional weight (+15kg, +20kg, +30kg) during booking.

✈ Can I change my seat or cancel my reservation?

Yes! Visit Manage Booking and enter your PNR to change seats or request flight cancellation.

⭐ How do AeroRewards loyalty points work?

Registered users automatically earn 10 AeroRewards points for every ₹100 spent on confirmed flight bookings. Reach 3,000 points for Silver tier, 10,000 points for Gold tier, and 25,000 points for Platinum tier.
